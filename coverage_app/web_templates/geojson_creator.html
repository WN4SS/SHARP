GeoJSON Scenario Creator

Search for a city or location to open the map and start building your coverage scenario.

How to use

  1. Pan the map to find your area of interest.
  2. Use the drawing tools:
    • Marker → Place a base station.
    • Polygon / Rectangle / Circle → Draw your area of interest.
  3. For base stations you can add multiple antennas by clicking on the base station.
  4. Click on the area drawn to view its properties.
  5. When done, click Save GeoJSON to store the scenario.
